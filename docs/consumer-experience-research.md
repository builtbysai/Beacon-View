# Consumer experience research and adopted patterns

Reviewed during the September 2026 local production-readiness work. These findings
guide Beacon's own interface; no competitor branding, images, or recordings are used.

| Source                                                                                                                                                                        | Useful pattern                                                                                                           | Beacon decision                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [AlfredCamera camera management](https://support.alfred.camera/hc/en-us/articles/360034803312-How-do-I-manage-my-Cameras)                                                     | Paired cameras appear in a camera list with entry points to viewing and management.                                      | Keep cameras primary on Home; put maintenance in camera details.                                                                                                                                                              |
| [AlfredCamera Live](https://support.alfred.camera/hc/en-us/articles/360039515892-Live)                                                                                        | Preview surfaces make cameras easy to scan.                                                                              | Use stable card proportions and explicit status. Automatic preview streams are deferred because they increase phone capture, encoding, and network demand. Stored thumbnails require a separate consent and retention design. |
| [Google Nest Talk and Listen](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/9219455?hl=en)                                                                                     | Explicit microphone interaction and audible conversation cues.                                                           | Keep independent Listen and Talk, visible microphone state, and Android start/end tones. Never restart the viewer microphone automatically after an interruption.                                                             |
| [Google Home camera controls](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/9241220?hl=en-C) and [camera alert settings](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/9247651) | Camera grids surface a name, availability state, and direct entry to the view; alert controls are scoped to each camera. | Home uses status-first camera cards with a direct live-view action, and camera-specific controls remain in the camera context. Beacon does not auto-start preview streams or claim that a symbolic card is a current image.   |
| [Google Home camera off](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/17073748)                                                                                               | Turning a camera off is explained as stopping streaming and sensing.                                                     | Privacy mode is presented as an immediate capture stop, ahead of availability, so an old online signal can never suggest capture is still active.                                                                             |
| [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and [focus order guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-order)                                                 | Predictable keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, and usable targets.                                   | Five mobile destinations, a skip link, current-page semantics, focus on the destination heading, at least 44-pixel primary targets, and automated light/dark text-contrast checks.                                            |

The dashboard uses the existing household inventory only. Privacy, revoked access,
disablement, and offline state take precedence over stale live flags. The Android
health contract now permits a current battery/charging sample; signal, historical
health, and a recent-activity count are still not invented. Camera cards deliberately
use an abstract lens surface rather than a stale image or an automatic preview stream.

Automated contrast checks cover the semantic normal-text/status palette and primary
action labels. They do not establish full WCAG conformance. Screen-reader testing,
all component states, Android TalkBack, large fonts, and the supported-browser matrix
remain release acceptance work.
