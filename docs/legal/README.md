# Beacon legal and trust package

Status: **draft for owner and qualified legal review; do not publish yet.**

This package is grounded in the current implementation, not an aspirational feature
list. It is written for a US-only, adults-only private alpha as the lowest-risk
starting scope. It must be completed with the operator's legal identity, contact
details, launch geography, and approved retention decisions before it appears in the
app, website, Google Play listing, or marketing.

## Documents

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md): public-facing privacy notice.
- [Terms of service](terms-of-service.md): public-facing service terms.
- [Acceptable use and camera notice](acceptable-use-and-camera-notice.md): lawful-use,
  notice, and consent rules for a camera/microphone product.
- [Data retention and deletion](data-retention-and-deletion.md): public-facing summary
  plus implementation gaps that prevent publication.
- [Support and privacy-request procedure](support-and-privacy-requests.md): internal
  response procedure and public contact routes.
- [Data inventory and subprocessors](data-inventory-and-subprocessors.md): current
  processing record for review and vendor management.
- [Google Play data-safety workbook](google-play-data-safety-workbook.md): factual
  worksheet, not a submission.
- [Security overview](security-overview.md): cautious public trust copy.
- [Launch decisions](launch-decisions.md): owner decisions and blockers.
- [Research basis](research-basis.md): authoritative sources consulted.

## Publication rule

Do not replace bracketed fields with guesses. A policy is not a substitute for a
working account-deletion flow, defined audit/log retention, incident response plan, or
appropriate professional advice for the jurisdictions in which Beacon is offered.
