# Beacon acceptable use and camera notice

Status: Draft public-facing notice. Publish with the Terms only after legal review.

## Use cameras and microphones lawfully

Only use Beacon with a device and location you own or are authorized to monitor. Give
clear notice and obtain consent whenever law requires it. Do not place a camera or use
audio in bathrooms, bedrooms used by guests, changing areas, or any other place where
people reasonably expect privacy. Do not use Beacon for hidden surveillance, harassment,
stalking, discrimination, or monitoring workers, tenants, guests, or children without
the permissions, notices, and consents required where you are.

Audio laws differ significantly by location. Before enabling camera audio or two-way
talk, determine whether everyone who may be recorded or heard must consent. If unsure,
disable audio and seek local legal advice.

## Keep the device visible and controllable

Android Camera Mode uses a persistent system notification and local camera/microphone
indicators. Privacy mode stops local capture. The account owner can request Privacy
mode remotely, but a device that is offline cannot receive a remote command until it
reconnects. Anyone near the camera should be able to identify the device and use the
local controls.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use the Service to:

- break the law or another person's rights;
- capture or distribute media without required consent;
- attempt to access another household, account, device, or network;
- interfere with the Service, probe its security, or bypass access controls;
- use the Service as an emergency, life-safety, or professionally monitored alarm;
- upload malware, automate abusive requests, or resell access without written
  permission.

Report suspected misuse or a security issue to **[SUPPORT / SECURITY EMAIL]**.
